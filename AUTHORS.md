## Licensing

As listed above, the libretro cores `snes9x` and `genesis_plus_gx` may not be sold or put in a commercial product. See the discussion about this at [#52](https://github.com/RobLoach/nextcloud-arcade/issues/52).

Additionally, the above libretro cores used are separate works by their own authors, shipped unmodified, and aren't covered under this application's AGPL licence. The full text of every one of their licences is available in [`img/cores/README.md`](img/cores/README.md) and [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/). [REUSE.toml](REUSE.toml) also covers each.

No copyrighted games are included or distributed in this application.
